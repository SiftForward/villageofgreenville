Boards and Commissions


Are You Interested in being on one of our Boards, Committees or Commissions?
Simply fill out an Application/Volunteer Form and return to the Village Clerk's Office.  When a position becomes available, your application will be considered for appointment.  

VILLAGE BOARD

Greenville's Village Board consists of a Village President and four Village Trustees.  Each term is 2 years with no limit on the number of terms a person can serve. The Board meets on the second Monday of each month at 5:30 p.m. at the Village Hall.  Meeting notices are posted at the Village Hall and on the Village's website.
